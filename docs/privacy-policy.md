# Privacy Policy for Image Extractor

Last updated: 2026-10-25.

## Local processing

Image Extractor reads image addresses and relevant page metadata from webpages you choose, or HTML files you import. It processes image URLs, page titles and URLs, selections and download results to extract images, apply supported original-URL rules, create ZIP archives and recover interrupted tasks. This processing runs on your device.

The extension does not send this information to a developer-operated server. It includes no analytics, advertising, payment service or account system.

## Requests to source websites

Previews, user-requested webpage loading and downloads contact the relevant source websites. These sites receive ordinary network request information, including your IP address. Cookies may accompany requests under your browser settings and the website's policies. The extension does not bypass website authentication or verification. The source websites' own privacy policies apply to their handling of requests.

## Local storage and retention

Preferences and download history use browser-local extension storage. Per-page extraction state also uses browser session storage. Image bytes and temporary ZIP data are stored locally to support saving and recovery; Chrome sync storage is not used.

Completed task-history cleanup targets records older than seven days and keeps at most twenty finished task chains. Cleanup occurs during later operations rather than on a guaranteed schedule. Active, recoverable or otherwise protected tasks and their caches can remain longer; browser storage limits can also remove cached data sooner. Recovery depends on available complete files and is not guaranteed.

You can remove the extension to clear its extension-managed browser storage. Removing the extension does not delete images or ZIP files you saved to your Downloads folder or elsewhere; delete those separately if desired. You can also revoke optional site and download permissions through Chrome.

## Data use

The developer does not sell user data, use it for advertising or unrelated purposes, or use it for creditworthiness or lending. Source-site requests are necessary to display or save the images you choose.

## Policy updates

This policy will be updated when the extension's data practices change. The published policy will show its revision date.

## Development feature: image-host page lookup

Version 1.1.0 can request recognized image-host detail pages when you enable original matching and grant optional site access. It reads their HTML locally to locate an image address; the host receives ordinary request information and cookies under browser policy. Fetched scripts are not executed.
